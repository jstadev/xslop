Questions

Is xslop part of X?

No. xslop is independent and has no relation with X Corp. The x in the name means crossed out. Community Notes is a feature of X; xslop only borrows the idea of a rule that needs both sides.

Do I log in, or give xslop my X account?

No. xslop uses no X API and no X login. It works inside the x.com page that you already have open.

What if I mark a real person by mistake?

Click Undo on the hidden line, or remove the account from your list in the xslop popup. Your vote goes away with it.

Can a group vote someone onto the list?

Votes from one side alone do not confirm an account. Marks from new installs wait for a person, and an automatic listing also needs voters with standing and posts that the server checked. The rules explain it in full.

Why do I install from a zip?

The zip lets you use xslop now, without a store listing. When a store listing is live, the download page shows its button.

Does it work on my phone?

Turn on mute sync on your computer. xslop then mutes confirmed accounts on your X account, slowly and at most 40 a day, so the X app hides them too. Firefox for Android can run the extension itself once its add-on listing is live.

Which accounts does mute sync mute, and can I undo it?

Your own slop list first, then confirmed accounts from the shared list. xslop keeps a record of its own mutes and never touches an account that you muted yourself. Undo all xslop mutes is one click in the popup.

My account is on the list. What now?

Check your handle and send an appeal. You get a code for your X bio, and an admin checks it by hand.
