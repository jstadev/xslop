Step 1 of 3

xslop needs access to x.com and to its own server to work. Your browser asks you once.

Your browser did not ask. Open its extension settings and allow xslop on x.com.

One click does three things

  1. Hides the accountEvery post and reply folds to one line.
  2. Mutes it on XThe X app on your phone hides it too.
  3. Adds one voteNew votes wait for a check before they change the shared list.

Two choices, both yours

Both are off. xslop works without them.

You are ready

Open x.com. Each reply and post gets a small xslop button next to like and repost. Click it on slop. Each hidden post shows Show and Undo, or Not slop for accounts from the shared list. Undo also removes the mute.

What xslop sends

  • Your votes. Slop or not slop, the account handle, the post id, whether it is a reply or a post, the id of the post that it answers and the time of the post. Never the text. The account page on x-slop.com can link to these posts as evidence, after the server checks that the account wrote them.
  • Signals, only if you turn them on. For each reply on a post page that you open: the handle of its account, its post id and the id of the post that it answers. Nothing else. The server does not store which install sent a signal.
  • A random key. xslop makes a key on this device and signs each vote with it. When xslop registers, it sends the public part of the key and the xslop version. The key has no name, no email and no link to your X account.
  • Never sent: your X login or cookies, your own handle, the people you follow, your mutes, and any page outside X.

The server does not store your IP address. It keeps a short hash of your network range to slow down fake installs. If your own account is on the list, you can appeal at x-slop.com.

xslop has no relation to X Corp. The x in the name means crossed out.
